About Us

Built for South Sudan.
Designed to Scale.

Paragon Development Ltd is a South Sudanese privately held investment and development company, incorporated in Juba in 2019. We operate across five high-impact sectors, bringing institutional-grade execution to one of Africa's most dynamic frontier markets.

Our firm combines deep local knowledge with international standards — backed by leadership that has worked at the highest levels of African development finance, regional integration, and public policy.

Institutional Experience.

Paragon's founder brings over two decades of leadership across multilateral development finance, African intergovernmental organisations, government policy, and private sector enterprise. This convergence of public and private sector experience is rare — and it defines how Paragon operates.

From coordinating 12-nation economic programmes at the ICGLR to advising on national budgets as Chief Policy Adviser to South Sudan's Minister of Finance, our leadership brings board-room calibre governance — including active engagement within Africa's multilateral development finance architecture — to every engagement.
